## What stays the same in every theme

The sticker mechanics (keyline + flat offset shadow, press behavior, hand-placed tilts), white text on every category chip, and the reserved-action rule — each theme's action color only ever means "do the thing" and always carries its theme's dark ink. Category hues are now tinted per theme, but each category keeps its hue family (REFLECT is always a blue, GOSSIP is always a red) so entries stay recognizable when you switch themes.
